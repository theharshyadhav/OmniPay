# OmniPay Terms of Use - DRAFT (not reviewed by counsel; do not publish as-is)

> Placeholder product name. Every legal statement below is a draft to be reviewed by qualified counsel in each launch jurisdiction.

## 1. What OmniPay charges
OmniPay charges **no fee, no conversion fee and no spread** to the person paying or the person being paid. When you pay, you pay the invoice's mid-market value in the asset you choose, rounded to that asset's smallest unit. If the payee accepts your asset, it moves as-is; otherwise OmniPay converts it into the payee's settlement currency at mid-market.

## 2. Costs that are not OmniPay's
Moving money **into** or **out of** OmniPay uses third-party services (for example PayPal). Their fees, limits, holds, currency rules and availability apply, are set by them, and are **outside OmniPay's pricing**. We do not claim that the whole chain from your bank to the payee's bank is free.

## 3. How OmniPay earns money
Only from promoters, who pay a share of completed payments for placement in the "pay with" list. Promoted rows are always labelled **Sponsored**, are never pre-selected, never change your price, and never override your region's rules, what the payee accepts, or safety limits.

## 4. Simulated assets
In this version crypto and shares are **simulated**: no real asset is transferred. Rows are marked **Simulated**. Shares only ever move to a payee who opted in and are never sold on anyone's behalf.

## 5. The assistant
The AI assistant acts only within limits you set (per-payment and daily caps, allowed assets, payee allow-list, confirmation threshold). It cannot raise them, cannot confirm its own payments, and every action is logged for you to review. AI output can be wrong; you remain responsible for payments you approve.

## 6. Availability, regions, risk
Features differ by country. We may switch assets, regions or the whole service off at any time for safety. Asset values can change between quote and payment; quotes expire after about 30 seconds.

## 7. To be completed by counsel
Eligibility and KYC, who holds balances and under what licence, safeguarding, disputes and chargebacks, liability, governing law, privacy and AI-provider data processing, complaints handling, promoter advertising terms.
